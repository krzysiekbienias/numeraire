Those two numbers answer different questions, and only the second one grades the model. A single draw is not supposed to land on the realized curve — one path out of a distribution has no reason to, so a large gap there means nothing. What the draw is good for is family resemblance: the contracts move as a curve rather than each on their own, and the seasonal shape survives the trip. Whether the model was right is the band's job: if the widths are honest roughly nineteen settles in twenty should fall inside it.
